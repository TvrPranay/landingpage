The problem

Too much content. No direction.

Endless tutorials and no idea what’s next. Most learners don’t quit because it’s hard. They quit because they can’t see the road.

The approach

Learning, as a guided game.

Ten levels with clear goals and real projects. Each one unlocks the next, until what you’ve built is ready for the job market.
